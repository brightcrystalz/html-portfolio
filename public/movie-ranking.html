The Best Movies According to Zainab

My Top 3 movies of all-time

Start-Up

Start-Up is an inspiring drama that follows the journey of young entrepreneurs in the competitive world of start-ups. The story is both heartwarming and motivational, showcasing the challenges and triumphs of pursuing one's dreams. The performances are stellar, and the writing is sharp and engaging.

Legend of the Blue Sea

Legend of the Blue Sea is a captivating South Korean drama that combines romance, fantasy, and adventure. The chemistry between the leads, Jun Ji-hyun and Lee Min-ho, is electric, and the storyline is both unique and engaging.

30 But 17

The story follows a woman who, after a traumatic event, wakes up in her 30-year-old body but with the mind of a 17-year-old. The performances are touching, and the narrative is both funny and poignant.
